# Beautiful UI

The components in this folder are ported to Vue from Beautiful UI
(https://www.beautifului.dev), "crafted primitives for AI-native interfaces", and dressed in the
Sway theme (`assets/ai.css`). Their shapes, motion and behaviour are Beautiful UI's; the data,
the wording and the wiring to the house are Sway Home's.
